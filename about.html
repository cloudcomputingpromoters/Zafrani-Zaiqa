Our Story

Born from a Love of
Hyderabadi Heritage

Zafrani Zaiqa — meaning "the taste of saffron" — was born from a simple conviction: the most memorable meals are the ones made with authentic recipes, real ingredients, and genuine care. What started as cooking for family gatherings quickly grew into a calling.

Our kitchen carries the culinary legacy of Hyderabad — a city whose food culture is as rich and layered as its history. Every biryani is dum-cooked the traditional way. Every Mandi is smoked low and slow. Every mittha is made just as our grandmothers taught us.

As a home-based kitchen, we pour our heart into every tray we prepare. We don't cut corners. We don't compromise on ingredients. And we never sacrifice the soul of a recipe for the sake of convenience.

What We Stand For

Our Values

🌿

Only Real Ingredients

No premixed spice packets. No artificial flavoring. Every spice is handpicked, every paste is freshly ground, and every ingredient is sourced with intention.

✅

Certified Halal

Every protein — chicken, mutton, or beef — is sourced from certified halal suppliers. We take this seriously, because you trust us with your family's food.

❤️

Made with Love

We cook every tray as if we're cooking for our own family. The care, the patience, the attention to detail — it all goes into every dish we prepare.

🕰️

Fresh to Order

We never pre-cook and store. Every order is prepared fresh on the day of delivery, ensuring maximum flavor, freshness, and food safety.

🤝

Community First

We're proud to serve our local South Asian community with food that feels like home — especially on Eid, weddings, and special occasions.

🌟

Authentic Recipes Only

Our recipes are generations old. We don't modernize them to take shortcuts — we honor them, because the original is already perfect.
