How Catering Works

1

Choose Your Menu

Browse our full menu and select the dishes and tray sizes that work best for your event size and preferences.

2

Contact Us to Order

Reach out via phone, WhatsApp, or our contact form. Provide event date, headcount, and desired dishes.

3

Confirm & Deposit

We'll send a quote within 24 hours. Confirm your order with a 50% deposit at least 48 hours before the event.

Fresh Delivery

On the day of your event, your trays are delivered hot and fresh — ready to be set up and served. Enjoy!
